RBI GUIDELINES FOR RETAIL LENDING — LOANFLOW REFERENCE DOCUMENT
Source: Reserve Bank of India Master Directions on Consumer Credit
Last updated: 2024

=== SECTION 1: LOAN ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA ===

1.1 Age Requirements
Borrowers must be at least 21 years of age at the time of loan application. The maximum age at loan maturity should not exceed 65 years for salaried individuals and 70 years for self-employed borrowers.

1.2 Income Verification
Lenders must verify income through salary slips (minimum 3 months), bank statements (minimum 6 months), or ITR (minimum 2 years for self-employed). Declared income must be validated against documentary evidence before sanctioning.

1.3 Debt-to-Income Ratio (DTI)
The total EMI obligations (including the proposed loan) must not exceed 50% of the net monthly income for salaried borrowers. For self-employed borrowers the limit is 60% of verified monthly income. Existing EMIs must be factored into DTI calculation.

1.4 Minimum Net Monthly Income
Salaried borrowers: ₹20,000 per month minimum.
Self-employed / business: ₹25,000 per month verified income.
These thresholds apply to Tier-2 cities and above. Rural/semi-urban thresholds may be reduced by up to 20%.

1.5 Employment Stability
Salaried: Minimum 6 months at current employer; total work experience of 2 years.
Self-employed: Minimum 2 years in current business with ITR proof.
Contract workers and gig economy workers require enhanced scrutiny.

=== SECTION 2: CREDIT ASSESSMENT ===

2.1 Credit Score Thresholds
Loans may be sanctioned as per the following credit score bands:
- 750 and above: Prime segment, lowest risk, best rates
- 700–749: Near-prime, standard rates apply
- 650–699: Sub-prime, enhanced due diligence required, higher interest rate
- Below 650: High risk, loan should generally be declined unless secured

2.2 Credit Bureau Pull
All loan applications must trigger a credit bureau inquiry (CIBIL, Equifax, Experian, or CRIF Highmark). The bureau score must be recorded in the loan file. Soft inquiries are permitted for pre-qualification; hard inquiries are required before sanction.

2.3 Repayment History Assessment
Any default in the last 24 months is a red flag. Settlements, write-offs, or NPA accounts in the credit history must be disclosed to the credit committee. Accounts with DPD (Days Past Due) of more than 30 days in the last 12 months require mandatory escalation.

2.4 Willful Default
Borrowers classified as willful defaulters by any institution must not be sanctioned new credit under any circumstances.

=== SECTION 3: INTEREST RATE REGULATIONS ===

3.1 Transparency Requirements
Lenders must disclose the Annual Percentage Rate (APR) prominently, including all fees, processing charges, and insurance premiums. The APR must be calculated and disclosed before disbursement.

3.2 Interest Rate Caps
Microfinance loans: Maximum interest rate capped at 22% p.a. (RBI circular FIDD.CO.Plan.BC.54/12.09.001/2021-22).
Personal loans under ₹1 lakh: Effective interest rate must not exceed 30% p.a. inclusive of all charges.
Consumer durable loans: Maximum rate 24% p.a.

3.3 Prepayment Rules
For floating rate personal loans, no prepayment penalty may be charged.
For fixed rate loans, prepayment penalty must not exceed 3% of the outstanding principal.
Borrowers must be informed of prepayment rights at the time of sanction.

3.4 Processing Fees
Processing fees must be disclosed upfront. They must not exceed 2% of the loan amount. Lenders may not charge processing fees on rejected applications unless the applicant has explicitly agreed to a fee for credit appraisal.

=== SECTION 4: KYC REQUIREMENTS ===

4.1 Mandatory KYC Documents
Proof of Identity: Aadhaar card, PAN card, Passport, Voter ID, or Driving License.
Proof of Address: Utility bill (not older than 3 months), Aadhaar, Passport, or bank statement.
PAN card is mandatory for all loan applications above ₹50,000.

4.2 Video KYC
RBI permits Video-based Customer Identification Process (V-CIP) as an alternative to in-person KYC. V-CIP must include live photo, PAN verification, and liveness check. V-CIP sessions must be recorded and stored for 5 years.

4.3 Re-KYC
Existing customers must complete re-KYC every 2 years for high-risk customers, every 8 years for medium-risk, and every 10 years for low-risk customers.

4.4 PAN Verification
All loan applicants must provide PAN. PAN must be verified against ITD (Income Tax Department) database. Loans above ₹10 lakh require linking Aadhaar with PAN.

=== SECTION 5: DISBURSEMENT RULES ===

5.1 Account Credit
Loan amounts must be credited only to the borrower's verified bank account. Third-party account disbursement is prohibited. NEFT/RTGS/IMPS must be used for all disbursements above ₹10,000.

5.2 Same-Day Disbursement
For pre-approved and fully documented applications, disbursement must happen within 24 hours of sanction. For new applications, maximum 3 working days from sanction.

5.3 Disbursement Confirmation
A disbursement advice must be sent to the borrower via SMS and email within 30 minutes of credit. The advice must include: amount disbursed, EMI start date, first EMI amount, and loan account number.

5.4 Post-Disbursement Monitoring
Lenders must monitor repayments for the first 90 days. Any delay beyond 30 days must trigger an automated outreach. Accounts classified as SMA-0 (31-60 DPD) must be escalated to the collections team.

=== SECTION 6: GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL ===

6.1 Internal Ombudsman
All NBFCs with assets above ₹5,000 crore must appoint an Internal Ombudsman. Customer complaints must receive a first response within 48 hours and full resolution within 30 days.

6.2 RBI Integrated Ombudsman Scheme
Borrowers may escalate unresolved complaints to the RBI Integrated Ombudsman. Lenders must cooperate with ombudsman proceedings and comply with awards within 30 days.

6.3 Fair Practices Code
Lenders must display the Fair Practices Code prominently on their website and branches. The code must cover: loan application process, appraisal timeline, interest rate disclosure, grievance contact details, and cooling-off period.
