LOANFLOW INTERNAL LENDING POLICY — CREDIT UNDERWRITING STANDARDS
Version: 3.2 | Effective: 2024-01-01 | Owner: Credit Risk Committee

=== SECTION 1: PRODUCT PARAMETERS ===

1.1 Personal Loan
Loan amount: ₹1,000 to ₹10,00,000 (₹10 lakh)
Tenure: 6 to 60 months
Interest rate: 10.5% to 28% p.a. (risk-based pricing)
Processing fee: Up to 2% of loan amount (minimum ₹500)
Prepayment charges: Nil for floating rate; 2% for fixed rate after 6 months
Target segment: Salaried and self-employed individuals aged 21–60

1.2 Loan Against Salary (LAS)
Loan amount: Up to 10x net monthly salary, capped at ₹5,00,000
Tenure: 12 to 48 months
Eligibility: Minimum take-home salary ₹25,000 per month
Processing fee: 1% flat
Interest rate: 11.5% to 22% p.a.

1.3 Business Loan (MSME)
Loan amount: ₹1,00,000 to ₹50,00,000
Tenure: 12 to 84 months
Eligible entities: Proprietorships, Partnerships, LLPs, Private Limited Companies
Minimum vintage: 2 years in business
Processing fee: 1.5% to 2.5%
Interest rate: 12% to 24% p.a.

=== SECTION 2: CREDIT SCORING MODEL ===

2.1 Risk Grade Classification
LoanFlow uses an internal risk grading system based on CIBIL score and supplementary factors:

Grade A (Score 750–900): Prime borrower
- Rate range: 10.5%–14% p.a.
- Loan amount: Up to 100% of eligible amount
- Special offers possible; fast-track processing

Grade B (Score 700–749): Standard borrower
- Rate range: 14%–18% p.a.
- Loan amount: Up to 90% of eligible amount
- Standard processing; no special offers

Grade C (Score 650–699): Sub-prime borrower
- Rate range: 18%–24% p.a.
- Loan amount: Up to 75% of eligible amount
- Enhanced documentation required; credit committee review

Grade D (Score 600–649): High-risk borrower
- Rate range: 24%–28% p.a.
- Loan amount: Up to 60% of eligible amount
- Mandatory credit committee approval; additional security may be required

Below 600: Decline recommended
- May be reconsidered if strong income evidence, strong employer, and low DTI

2.2 DTI Thresholds by Grade
Grade A: DTI up to 50% acceptable
Grade B: DTI up to 45% acceptable
Grade C: DTI up to 40% acceptable
Grade D: DTI up to 35% acceptable

2.3 Supplementary Scoring Factors
The following factors can adjust the base credit score rating by +/- 1 grade:
Positive adjustments:
- Stable employment at reputable employer 5+ years: +1 grade
- Home ownership: +0.5 grade
- Existing relationship with LoanFlow (no defaults): +0.5 grade
- Post-graduate education in professional field: +0.25 grade

Negative adjustments:
- Rented accommodation with frequent moves: -0.5 grade
- Self-employed less than 3 years: -0.5 grade
- Multiple loan inquiries in last 6 months (>4): -0.5 grade
- Employment gap in last 2 years (>3 months): -0.5 grade

=== SECTION 3: SANCTIONING AUTHORITY MATRIX ===

3.1 Automated Sanctioning (AI Pipeline)
Loans up to ₹2,00,000 (₹2 lakh) for Grade A and B borrowers may be sanctioned by the automated AI pipeline without human review.

3.2 Relationship Manager (RM) Review
Loans above ₹2,00,000 require RM review before sanction.
RM sanctioning authority: Up to ₹10,00,000 for Grade A borrowers, ₹5,00,000 for Grade B.
RM must document rationale for any deviation from credit model recommendation.

3.3 Credit Committee
Loans above RM authority, all Grade C/D loans, and all exceptions to policy require credit committee approval.
Credit committee meets every Tuesday and Thursday.
Emergency approvals: Available via 2 senior credit officers for time-sensitive cases.

3.4 Deviation Policy
Deviations from standard policy are permitted but must be:
- Documented with clear rationale
- Approved one level above the standard sanctioning authority
- Logged in the deviation register
- Reviewed quarterly by the Credit Risk Committee

=== SECTION 4: REPAYMENT COLLECTION POLICY ===

4.1 EMI Collection
EMIs are collected via e-NACH mandate on the EMI due date. Backup collection via payment link is available if NACH fails.

4.2 Grace Period
A 3-day grace period is permitted before an EMI is considered overdue. No late payment charges are levied within the grace period.

4.3 Overdue Charges
Overdue interest: 2% per month on the overdue EMI amount.
Bounce charges: ₹500 per NACH return, not to exceed 3 charges per month.

4.4 Collections Escalation
Days 1–30 (SMA-0): Automated SMS and email reminders; system calls.
Days 31–60 (SMA-1): Field executive outreach; payment plan discussion.
Days 61–90 (SMA-2): Legal notice; settlement offer at 90% of outstanding.
Day 91+ (NPA): Account transferred to NPA recovery team; legal action if required.

4.5 Waiver Policy
Principal waiver: Not permitted without board approval.
Interest/penalty waiver: Up to 50% may be waived by Credit Committee for settlement.
Settlement: One-time settlements accepted for NPA accounts at minimum 60% of outstanding principal.

=== SECTION 5: FRAUD DETECTION POLICY ===

5.1 Document Fraud Indicators
Red flags for document fraud:
- Inconsistent fonts or formatting in salary slips
- Bank statement debits not matching stated employer's sector
- ITR income significantly higher than industry average for stated profession
- Address inconsistency across documents
- Salary credits from an unknown/unregistered entity
- Round-number salary amounts (e.g., exactly ₹50,000 when market rate varies)

5.2 Identity Fraud Indicators
- PAN not matching name in credit bureau
- Date of birth inconsistency across documents
- Multiple applications from same mobile number with different identities
- IP address or device associated with known fraud patterns
- Application submitted from a high-fraud pin code

5.3 Collusion Fraud Indicators
- Same employer reference for multiple unrelated applicants
- Common guarantor across multiple applications
- Identical phone numbers or email addresses across applications
- Applications submitted in rapid succession from the same agent/branch

5.4 Fraud Decision Policy
Confirmed fraud: Immediate decline; report to FIU-IND; blacklist applicant.
Suspected fraud: Refer to fraud team; additional verification required.
Cleared: Document outcome and proceed with normal underwriting.

=== SECTION 6: LOAN RESTRUCTURING POLICY ===

6.1 Eligibility for Restructuring
Loans eligible for restructuring must meet:
- Account in SMA-1 or SMA-2 category (not yet NPA)
- Borrower must demonstrate temporary financial hardship (job loss, medical emergency, business disruption)
- Maximum one restructuring per loan

6.2 Restructuring Options
Option A: EMI holiday — 1 to 3 months, interest continues to accrue.
Option B: EMI reduction — Extend tenure to reduce EMI by up to 30%.
Option C: Interest rate reduction — Reduce rate by up to 2% for Grade A borrowers in hardship.

6.3 Post-Restructuring Monitoring
Restructured accounts must be monitored monthly. Any subsequent default within 12 months triggers NPA classification and legal proceedings.
